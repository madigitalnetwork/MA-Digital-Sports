Who we are

About

MA Digital Sports is a cricket coverage platform. The goal is simple — present every ball of a match clearly, quickly and in a way that is actually pleasant to look at.

What this site does

On the live page, the score, overs, partnership, batter strike rates and bowler economy all update themselves every few seconds. In the middle sits an animated stadium view where the shot played matches the outcome of the ball — a four, a six, or a wicket.

Where the data comes from

Scores are supplied by a third-party cricket data API. The server polls that API and hands ready-made data to the browser, so whether one person is watching or a thousand, the load on the API stays the same.

How it works

The server polls the APIEvery six seconds. The key lives on the server, never in the browser.
It works out the differenceComparing two updates tells us what happened on the ball in between.
The page updates itselfNumbers animate as they change and the stadium plays the matching shot.
